Global Feature Importance Weights

Relative statistical contribution of features in determining property value across all Indian cities.

Key Econometric Drivers
1. Locality & Metropolitan Tier (35-40%)

Metropolitan tiers (e.g. South Mumbai, DLF Cyber City, Indiranagar) establish the base square foot multiplier, commanding up to 5x higher baseline than outer perimeters.

2. Built-up Carpet Area (30-35%)

Linear scale multiplier. Larger area properties exhibit non-linear compounding in luxury micro-markets.

3. Room Configuration & Baths (12-15%)

Number of bedrooms and attached ensuite baths represent functional liveability thresholds in urban nuclear families.

4. Age & Depreciation (-8 to -18%)

New construction (0-2 years) commands a novelty premium; older structures (>12 years) exhibit steady physical depreciation.

5. Amenities & High-Rise Floor (5-8%)

High-rise floor elevation, gym, swimming pool, and dedicated parking bays provide an incremental compounding bump.
